## A. Overview & Positioning

### Who EBI Resources is
EBI Resources is a **holding company** that unites a portfolio of businesses under one corporate identity. Its purpose on this website is to present the group professionally, consolidate its business lines in one place, build credibility, and open the door to partnership, investment, and recruitment.

**Business lines (per README):**
1. **Hotels**
2. **Restaurants**
3. **Café**
4. **IT & Technology**
5. **Travel** (positioned as *Coming Soon* — in development)

### Value proposition (grounded synthesis)
EBI Resources develops and operates hospitality and lifestyle destinations that bring **the warmth of Indonesian hospitality to the Silk Road heritage of Uzbekistan**, anchored around Central Asia’s most significant Islamic heritage and pilgrimage sites — complemented by a growing technology practice and an emerging travel line.

> This value proposition is a distillation of themes that recur verbatim across the property research (e.g., “Where Samarkand’s heritage meets the warmth of Indonesian hospitality,” “Bringing the warmth of Indonesian hospitality to the heart of Samarkand,” “From Jakarta to Tashkent”). Keep the corporate-level claim thematic; do not attach invented scale figures (number of properties/countries/employees) unless the client supplies them.

### Brand pillars / themes (distilled from the portfolio)
These pillars are supported by multiple properties and are safe to lead with:

- **Indonesian hospitality, abroad.** Authentic Indonesian warmth, cuisine, and coffee culture delivered in Uzbekistan (present in every property: Hadith’s Indonesian service & Kopi Luwak, Mecca’s Indonesian signature restaurant, Kampoeng Indonesia’s village concept, Graha Nusantara’s Nusantara heritage, 7OZ’s “From Jakarta to Tashkent”).
- **Silk Road heritage & setting.** Samarkand and Tashkent locations tied to Uzbek craftsmanship and architecture.
- **Faith & heritage tourism.** Multiple properties sit at or beside the **Imam Al Bukhari Complex** (Samarkand) or the **Tashkent Centre of Islamic Civilization** — serving pilgrimage/ziyarah and halal travelers.
- **Halal-friendly by design.** Halal dining and faith amenities (e.g., qibla-marked rooms, prayer mats, prayer rooms) appear across properties.
- **A cross-cultural bridge (Indonesia ↔ Uzbekistan).** A recurring narrative of shared heritage and hospitality connecting the two nations.
- **Contemporary quality across tiers.** A portfolio spanning 5★, 4★ boutique, 3★, and luxury villa/residence formats, plus specialty café and (emerging) technology and travel lines.

### What NOT to claim at corporate level
- Do not state a total property count, country count, revenue, employee count, founding year, or “first/largest/only” superlatives unless the client confirms them.
- Do not present development-stage properties as fully operating (several source decks/sites read as pre-opening or early-build). Use neutral language (e.g., “part of our portfolio”) and confirm operating status with the client.

## B. Audience & Messaging Strategy

### Primary personas (lead the content for these)
| Persona | What they need to see | Content that serves them |
|---|---|---|
| **Potential business partners** | Credibility, scope of the group, tangible assets, clear point of contact | Portfolio breadth, real properties, professional presentation, partnership CTA |
| **Investors & stakeholders** | Scale and direction of the business, quality of assets, governance | Company overview, business lines, property portfolio, leadership, vision & mission |
| **IT & Technology clients** | Capability areas, applicable industries, how to engage | IT & Technology page: capabilities, target sectors, business contact |

### Secondary personas
Public/guests (hotels, restaurants, café), media & institutions, government/related organizations, and job seekers. Serve these without turning the site into a booking or e-commerce channel — link out or direct to contact instead.

### Tone of voice
- **Credible, partnership-oriented, and confident** — a corporate holding voice, not a promotional booking site.
- **Warm and human** where hospitality is described (reflecting the Indonesian-hospitality brand pillar).
- **Clear and factual** on the IT & Technology and corporate pages.
- Avoid hype, superlatives, and unverified statistics.

### Credibility signals to emphasize (factual only)
> **Lead with qualitative proof, not numbers (decided).** For the homepage specifically, do **not** use a numeric stat-band yet (see Section F.6). The signals below are qualitative and are the preferred way to build credibility at this stage.

- **A real, multi-property portfolio** across Samarkand and Tashkent (Hadith Hotel, Mecca Boutique Hotel & Restaurant, Graha Nusantara, Hotel Kampoeng Indonesia, plus the 7OZ Espresso café).
- **Prime locations** at/adjacent to globally significant landmarks (Imam Al Bukhari Complex; Tashkent Centre of Islamic Civilization).
- **Halal certification** referenced for dining/properties (certifying body to be confirmed).
- **Hotel Kampoeng Indonesia holds an Uzbekistan national tourism standard Certificate of Conformity** (O‘z DSt 3220:2023) — a concrete, verifiable credential. *(Certificate details in Section E; confirm the operating-entity name shown on the certificate before publishing — see Section H.)*
- **Notable visits** documented in property press (e.g., senior Indonesian and Malaysian figures visiting properties). *Use only with client sign-off and correct attribution; verify names/titles before publishing. Do not overstate as endorsements.*

> **Guardrail:** Awards, records, and certifications belonging to neighboring landmarks (e.g., Guinness World Records / UNESCO recognition associated with the Tashkent Centre of Islamic Civilization) belong to those landmarks — **not** to EBI or its hotels. Present them as “about the location,” never as EBI’s own credentials.
