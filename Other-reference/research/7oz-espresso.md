# 7oz Espresso — Business Context Research

**Source website:** https://7oz-espresso.com/
**Researched:** 2026-07-21
**Purpose:** Business-context research for the EBI Resources corporate-profile website (café/coffee brand line).

> **Method note:** Content below was scraped from the live site (home, about, menus, contact, blog, and sample product pages). Everything under "Facts from site" is copied/paraphrased directly from the site. Interpretation is clearly separated. Our journey across borders is driven by a simple mission: to bring the soul of Indonesian coffee culture to an international stage. ## 2. Corporate / ownership context (Facts from site — from Grand Opening blog)

The Grand Opening blog post states the corporate structure explicitly:

- Grand opening event held **Wednesday, April 15, 2026**, at **Tashkent Boulevard, Uzbekistan** ("one of the most premium and prestigious commercial areas").
- 7oz Espresso is described as *"one of the latest flagship business lines managed directly by **EGI Food**."*
- **EGI Food** = a **sub-holding** operating in the **food & beverage (F&B) and hospitality management** sectors.
- EGI Food operates *"under the large umbrella of its parent company, **PT. EGI Resources**."*
- **PT. EGI Resources** is described as known for *"investment and excellent corporate governance in various national-level strategic sectors."*
- 7oz Tashkent is described as EGI Resources' **first international branch** and its entry into the Central Asian / Uzbekistan market, based on market research into urban lifestyle and economic growth in the capital.

> ⚠️ **Important discrepancy to reconcile (Interpretation):** The website's blog consistently says **"EGI Food" / "PT. EGI Resources"**, whereas this project is named **"ebi-resources" / EBI Resources**. These may be the same entity under different transliteration/spelling, a rebrand, or a naming inconsistency on the café site. This should be verified with the client before publishing anything on the corporate site. Do **not** assume EGI = EBI without confirmation.

## 9. Interpretation / inference (clearly separated — NOT stated as fact)

- **Core narrative for the corporate site:** 7oz Espresso is the F&B/coffee flagship demonstrating the group's international expansion — an "Indonesia → Central Asia" story (Jakarta heritage, Tashkent execution). This is a strong, differentiated brand angle for the EBI Resources profile.
- **Real, verifiable facts** are essentially: the Tashkent café (address/phone/email/hours), the April 15 2026 grand opening, the menu categories, the differentiators, and the corporate chain (EGI Food → PT. EGI Resources). Much of the rest is template placeholder.
- The site is **early-stage / partially templated** (Lorem ipsum, dummy team, non-live store, mismatched product slugs). The corporate website should rely on the confirmed facts and request updated copy rather than reusing placeholders.
- **Multi-location branding (Jakarta/Samarkand/Bali)** likely signals ambition/heritage rather than four fully operational outlets — only Tashkent has published operating details.
- **Name reconciliation is a blocker:** EGI vs EBI must be confirmed before publishing corporate copy.
