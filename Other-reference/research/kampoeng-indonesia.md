# Hotel Kampoeng Indonesia — Samarkand: Business-Context Research

**Source:** Official website <https://hotel-kampoengindonesia.com/> (WordPress + Elementor site, `en-US`)
**Researched:** 2026-07-21
**Purpose:** Business-context research for EBI Resources corporate-profile website (property: Hotel Kampoeng Indonesia, Samarkand).

> **Method note:** Content was retrieved from the live site (home, benefits, about, reservation, reviews/testimonies, contact, and 6 news posts) plus raw HTML for structured data (schema, footer contact block). Pages fetched: Home, Benefits, About, Reservation, Reviews (`/testimonies/`), Contact, two testimonial detail pages, and six news articles. No `/restaurant/`, `/menu/`, or `/dining/` page exists (all returned 404). The site is small and partly a placeholder (see notes).

## 1. Property Overview

### Facts from site
- **Property name:** Hotel Kampoeng Indonesia (also written "Kampoeng Indonesia Hotel" / "Kampoeng Indonesia Samarkand Hotel"), Samarkand, Uzbekistan.
- **Tagline / hero headline:** "A JOURNEY OF FAITH AND HERITAGE: HOTEL KAMPOENG INDONESIA".
- **Star rating:** Described in news posts as a **three-star hotel** ("The three-star hotel is one of Indonesia's investments in Uzbekistan").
- **Concept/positioning:** "We bring the authentic charm of an Indonesian village (*Kampoeng*) to this historic site, offering a soulful stay that honors tradition while providing modern luxury for the discerning traveler." It markets itself as "cultural elegance located at the heart of the Imam Al Bukhari Memorial."
- **Benefits page theme:** "AUTHENTIC KAMPOENG EXPERIENCE — Discover the genuine warmth of traditional Indonesian village hospitality. We bring the architectural beauty and communal spirit of a Kampoeng to life within a luxurious setting."
- **Story / theme:** Indonesian village charm + hospitality ("keramahan khas Nusantara") set at a historic Islamic pilgrimage site; a "peaceful sanctuary after your day of pilgrimage or exploration."
- **Language/site status:** Default English; testimonial detail pages include long-form Indonesian text. `openingHours` in schema listed as `Mon–Sun 09:00–17:00` (this is a generic schema value, likely office/reception, not a hotel check-in window).

### Interpretation / inference
- The brand hook is a differentiated cultural niche: an "Indonesian kampoeng" experience physically embedded in a major Islamic heritage site — aimed squarely at Indonesian/Malaysian Muslim pilgrims who want familiar food, language, and ambiance abroad.
- "Modern luxury" language coexists with a stated 3-star rating; positioning is more "authentic/comfortable cultural boutique" than true luxury.

## 5. Restaurant / Dining  *(Special Focus)*

> **There is NO dedicated restaurant, menu, or dining page on the website** (`/restaurant/`, `/menu/`, `/dining/` all return 404). Dining is represented only by a generic **"Food & Beverages"** amenity icon on the Home and Benefits pages. The substantive dining evidence comes from **news posts and guest testimonials**, not a dedicated F&B page.

### Facts from site (F&B evidence, verbatim/paraphrased)
- **Amenity:** "Food & Beverages" listed as a hotel feature (no outlet name, cuisine list, hours, or menu given).
- **Cuisine = authentic Indonesian.** Testimonial from H. Ahmad Muzani: *"It offers an authentic Indonesian atmosphere and **delicious Indonesian food.** I highly recommend staying here."*
- **On-site luncheon / dining capability:** From the Muzani visit article — *"Ahmad Muzani and his delegation enjoyed a **luncheon** at the Kampoeng Indonesia Hotel. He expressed his appreciation for the flavors of the dishes served, noting that they successfully presented **authentic Indonesian cuisine** right in the middle of Uzbekistan's religious tourism area."* He also "commended the hotel's ambiance, which he felt truly captured an Indonesian atmosphere."
- **Operating entity is F&B-named:** The hotel is operated/managed by **"EGI Foods and Beverages MChJ"** (the legal entity named on its Uzbekistan certificate — see §7), signaling F&B is central to the business.
- **HORECA focus:** The Deputy Trade Minister article frames the property within Indonesia's push to export products to Uzbekistan "particularly in the **HORECA (hotel, restaurant, and cafe) sector**."

### Interpretation / inference
- The hotel clearly serves Indonesian food on-site (able to host a ministerial luncheon), but has **not branded or documented a named restaurant outlet** on the website. Cuisine style, menu highlights, ambiance descriptors, and opening hours are **not published** — only "authentic Indonesian cuisine / atmosphere" as a repeated theme.
- Given the operating company name ("Foods and Beverages") and the HORECA-export narrative, dining/Indonesian F&B appears to be a core strategic pillar of the venture, even though it is under-represented on the current website. This is an obvious content opportunity for the EBI corporate profile.

- Subject to periodic inspection/supervision during the validity period.
