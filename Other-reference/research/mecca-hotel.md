## 1. Property Overview

### Facts stated in document
- **Property name:** Mecca Hotel — also styled **"Mecca Boutique Hotel & Restaurant"** and **"Mecca Boutique Hotel Tashkent."**
- **Positioning / tagline lines used in deck:**
  - "Boutique Hotel & Restaurant"
  - "A boutique address for the new spiritual capital of Central Asia."
  - "Compact in scale, deliberate in position."
- **Star rating shown on cover:** ★★★★ (four stars).
- **Concept / classification:** Boutique hotel (explicitly "Positioning: Boutique Hotel" on the fact sheet).
- **Core story / theme:** The hotel has **30 rooms inspired by the 30 Juz of the Holy Qur'an**; each room "reflects the values of peace, completeness, and spiritual harmony."
- **Owner / leadership quote:** Welcome letter signed by **Erslan Ibrahim, Chairman & CEO.**
- **Guest use cases named:** business, leisure, cultural exploration, and pilgrimage.
- **Target framing:** "Built for the Asia halal traveller, the regional business guest, and the pilgrim alike."

### Interpretation / inference
- The name "Mecca" plus the Qur'an-Juz room concept and adjacency to an Islamic landmark position this squarely as a **faith-aligned / halal-hospitality boutique product**, not a generic business hotel.
- The "EGI Property Nusantara 01" cover label suggests this is the **first property in a numbered portfolio/series** ("01"), likely under an EBI/EGI-linked holding structure. The deck does not explain the EGI–EBI relationship.

## 2. Location & Setting

### Facts stated in document
- **City / country:** Tashkent, Uzbekistan (Central Asia).
- **Key location claim:** Located **directly opposite the Tashkent Centre of Islamic Civilization** (a.k.a. Islamic Civilization Centre) — described as "the closest hospitality address."
- **Frontage / tenure:** Freehold title with "prominent boulevard frontage."
- **Proximity figures:**
  - **~30 min** to Tashkent International Airport.
  - **20 min** to Tashkent Railway Station.
- **Room views:** Directed either toward the Islamic Civilization Centre or the inner garden.

### About the adjacent landmark (Tashkent Centre of Islamic Civilization) — as stated
- Uzbekistan's **largest cultural landmark**; Uzbekistan's flagship cultural landmark.
- Spans **50,000 m²** built-up (also described as **10 hectares**), with a **65-meter central dome.**
- **Certified by Guinness World Records** as the largest Islamic museum.
- **Estimated investment: US$160 million.**
- Houses the **original Uthman Qur'an manuscript / "Original Uthman Mushaf"** — one of the world's oldest Qur'an manuscripts, **recognized by UNESCO.**
- Also features a museum, library, research institute, and exhibition galleries.

### Interpretation / inference
- The entire commercial thesis rests on **landmark adjacency**: the hotel markets itself primarily as the nearest, best-positioned lodging to a major (and heavily promoted) Islamic tourism destination.
- The two proximity figures (airport, railway) imply reasonable transit access, but no detail is given on public transport or parking.

## 3. Building, Scale & Program

### Facts stated in document
- **Land parcel:** 3,000 m² (freehold).
- **Total built-up area:** 3,038.7 m² (headline sometimes rounded to "3,038 m²").
- **Structure:** 3 floors + rooftop (also described as "four functional levels").
- **Floor-by-floor breakdown (By the Numbers):**
  - **Basement — 531.8 m²:** Back-of-house, MEP, staff & storage.
  - **Ground floor — 854.7 m²:** Lobby, 15 guest rooms, reception, Indonesian restaurant.
  - **Second floor — 1,051.5 m²:** 15 guest rooms · Italian restaurant.
  - **Rooftop terrace — 600.7 m²:** Café & bar.
- **Headline program summary:** "Thirty guest rooms, three F&B venues, and a rooftop terrace."
- **Halal certification:** "100% Halal-Certified."

### Architectural identity (as stated)
- "Modern Islamic architecture" — a contemporary reinterpretation of Uzbek architectural language.
- Features: load-bearing brick, a portal entry, and decorative panels referencing Tashkent's historic **madrasas**, in "restrained modern proportions."
- "Three Storeys · Rooftop Terrace."

### Interpretation / inference
- This is a **compact, single-building, mixed-use hospitality asset** (rooms + three F&B venues) on a small footprint — the deck repeatedly leans on "compact/boutique" as a deliberate strategy rather than a limitation.

## 6. Restaurant(s) — Dedicated Section (SPECIAL FOCUS)

The hotel brands itself as a "Hotel & Restaurant" and operates **three F&B venues**. Two are full restaurants (Indonesian, Italian) and one is a rooftop café & bar. **All dining is halal-certified.**

### 6.1 Indonesian Restaurant (the hotel's signature restaurant)

**Facts stated in document**
- **Positioning:** The hotel's **signature dining room** / "signature restaurant." Called out as a competitive advantage: "Indonesian signature restaurant."
- **Cuisine:** Authentic Indonesian.
- **Concept/theme:** "An authentic Indonesian table" — "the full canon of Indonesian cuisine."
- **Menu highlights (named dishes):** **Rendang, Nasi Goreng, Sate (Satay), Soto, and Gado-Gado.**
- **Kitchen team:** Prepared by **Indonesian chefs.**
- **Standard:** Halal throughout ("prepared … to a halal standard").
- **Size / location:** **497 m²** of seating, on the **ground floor.**
- **Slide labels:** "497 m² · Ground Floor · Halal Certified."

**Interpretation / inference**
- This is the flagship F&B concept and a clear **brand differentiator** tied to the Indonesian ownership (EBI/EGI). It doubles as a draw for both hotel guests and local residents.
- No opening hours, seat count (vs. area), price point, or à la carte vs. buffet format are stated.

### 6.2 Italian Restaurant

**Facts stated in document**
- **Cuisine:** Authentic Italian ("Italian cuisine, on site").
- **Positioning:** "A distinctive offering in Central Asia and a strong attraction for international travelers seeking **premium European dining experiences.**"
- **Menu highlights (named dishes):** Timeless Italian classics — **fresh pasta, risotto, wood-fired pizza, osso buco, and tiramisu.**
- **Kitchen team:** A specialized culinary team / "Italian Kitchen Team."
- **Standard:** "100% halal — Certified · Traceable"; "prepared to full halal standards."
- **Size / location:** **452 m²** dedicated dining venue, on the **second floor (L2).**
- **Slide descriptors:** "Authentic Italian Menu · Italian Kitchen Team · Halal Certified."

**Interpretation / inference**
- Framed as a scarcity play — premium European/Italian dining is positioned as rare in Central Asia, appealing to international guests.
- No hours, seat count, or pricing stated.

### 6.3 Rooftop Café & Bar

**Facts stated in document**
- **Concept:** "Your open-air oasis in the heart of the city" — a rooftop terrace.
- **Size / location:** **600.7 m²** rooftop terrace — "the building's full footprint at sky level."
- **Views:** Unobstructed views toward the Islamic Civilization Centre's dome and Tashkent's modern skyline.
- **Offerings:** Serves **coffee, light meals, and shisha.**
- **Event use:** Bookable as an exclusive event venue.
- **Descriptor:** "600.7 m² · Rooftop Café & bar."

**Interpretation / inference**
- Aligns with EBI's "Café" business line. Note it is labeled "café & bar," but given the 100% halal positioning, "bar" likely refers to a beverage/coffee/shisha bar rather than an alcohol-led venue (the deck does not clarify alcohol policy).

### 6.4 Restaurant / Event Capacity

**Facts stated in document**
- Headline figure: **"500 — Restaurant Capacity"** and **"Restaurant Capacity 500"** for weddings, corporate functions, religious gatherings, and celebrations.
- Described as a "multifunctional event venue for up to 500 guests."

### 6.5 In-document inconsistency to flag (restaurants)
- Competitive-advantage slide #05 is titled **"Indonesian signature restaurant"** but its body text says the property features **"authentic Italian & Indonesian cuisine"** — i.e., the heading and body don't fully match. Treat both restaurants (Indonesian = signature; Italian = distinctive premium offering) as the two dining concepts.
- Minor typo in the Italian slide body ("he menu features…" — missing "T").

## 12. Data-Quality Notes & Flags

- **Nature of source:** Marketing/development profile deck; figures are self-reported and may reflect design intent rather than as-built/operating reality.
- **Possible pre-opening status:** Language ("programmed," "conceived," "built for") and the June/July 2026 date suggest the property may be **in development or newly opening** rather than long-operating. Not explicitly stated.
- **Inconsistencies flagged:** (a) Slide #05 heading "Indonesian signature restaurant" vs. body "Italian & Indonesian cuisine"; (b) "500 Restaurant Capacity" is also described as an "event venue for up to 500 guests" — the 500 figure is an event/banquet capacity, not necessarily simultaneous restaurant covers.
- **Typos in source:** "Taskhent" (for Tashkent) appears on multiple slides; "he menu features" (Italian slide).
- **EBI vs. EGI:** Task brief references "EBI Resources"; the deck's cover says "EGI Property Nusantara." The relationship between the two is not explained in the document.
